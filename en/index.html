E-commerce fulfilment in Serbia

A fulfilment centre that works as your warehouse, packing room and courier desk

PAKUM is a fulfilment centre (3PL) in Serbia for online stores. We take over everything that happens between the customer's click and the delivered parcel: receiving with verification and barcoding, warehousing, pick and pack with scan control, shipping through all Serbian couriers under one contract and one statement, cash-on-delivery collection with a fiscal receipt on every parcel, payout of collected amounts and returns handling. The TopOMS software comes with the service, so you see live stock and the status of every shipment.

We ship across Serbia, including Belgrade, Novi Sad and Niš. The same team, warehouse and software run our own brands and our clients' orders, which is why we say: we pack as if it were our own. If you are a brand from the region or further away and want to sell to Serbian customers, read how selling in Serbia from abroad works, or get a quote.

Brands from the EU, the UK, Switzerland, Turkey and the Western Balkans use PAKUM as their Serbian 3PL (also spelled fulfillment center): stock crosses the border once, customs-cleared, and every order ships locally with cash on delivery and a fiscal receipt. Customs, VAT, couriers and consumer rules are explained in our guide to fulfilment in Serbia for European brands.

FAQ

Fulfilment in Serbia: frequently asked questions

What is included in PAKUM fulfilment?

Receiving with verification and barcoding, warehousing, pick and pack with scan control, shipping through all Serbian couriers under one contract, cash-on-delivery collection with a fiscal receipt on every parcel, payout of collected amounts and returns handling. The TopOMS software is included.

Can a foreign brand use PAKUM to sell in Serbia?

Yes. Brands from the region and beyond keep stock in our warehouse in Serbia and we ship to Serbian customers with cash on delivery. The exact setup depends on whether you have a Serbian company or work through a local partner model; we go through the options on a call.

Which couriers do you ship with?

All major Serbian couriers, under one contract and one statement with us. You do not sign courier contracts yourself.

Do you connect to Shopify and WooCommerce?

Yes. Orders flow from your store into TopOMS, stock levels are sent back to the store, and you can follow every shipment's status in the software. Sales funnel platforms and an API are supported too.

How is pricing structured?

Per unit of work: receiving, storage by the space your goods occupy, packing per parcel, courier cost per shipment and cash-on-delivery handling. Every item is listed in the quote with its unit, VAT and validity.
