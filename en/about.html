About PAKUM

Fulfilment born from our own online selling

PAKUM did not start as a logistics company. It grew out of a Serbian e-commerce operation that sells its own brands online every day. The warehouse, team and software that ship our orders now ship our clients' orders too.

About PAKUM: frequently asked questions

Who is behind PAKUM?

PAKUM is the fulfilment brand of PAKUM DOO NIŠ, a Serbian e-commerce company that runs its own online brands and now offers the same warehouse, team and software to other stores.

Where is the warehouse?

In Serbia. The exact address is shared with clients and visitors by appointment; we ship across the whole country.

How do I contact you?

Through the quote form or by email at info@pakum.rs. We reply with a call to go through your needs.
