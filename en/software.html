TopOMS: your stock, orders and shipments in one place

TopOMS is the software our own brands run on and that every PAKUM client gets: live stock per product and variant, the status of every shipment from order to delivery, cash-on-delivery records, returns by reason and reports.

What you see

Everything about your goods, live

Stock

Physical and available stock per product and variant, with reserved quantities shown separately; every item carries a QR code.

Shipments

Status of every parcel from order to delivery, with courier statuses, and flags for customers who refuse parcels.

Money and returns

Cash-on-delivery records and payout statements, returns by reason, and reports on sales, returns and shipping costs.

Integrations

Shopify, WooCommerce, sales funnels and API

Your store connects once to TopOMS. Orders flow in automatically, stock levels flow back, and shipment statuses are visible in the software. Details for Shopify and WooCommerce stores are on the integration page.

FAQ

Software: frequently asked questions

Is TopOMS included in the price?

Yes, for fulfilment clients. Store connection and onboarding are listed separately in the quote.

Which stores can be connected?

Shopify, WooCommerce and sales funnel platforms directly, and other systems through the API. The order intake for other channels is agreed in the quote.

Can several people from my team use it?

Yes. Access is per user, so your team sees stock, orders and reports without asking us.
