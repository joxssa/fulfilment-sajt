FAQ

Shopify and WooCommerce: frequently asked questions

Do I have to change my store platform?

No. Your Shopify or WooCommerce store stays as it is; only who packs and ships changes. The store connects to TopOMS and orders flow to our warehouse.

Does stock sync back to the store?

Yes. TopOMS keeps physical and available stock per product and variant and sends it back to the store, so the store sells only what is on the shelf.

What about cash on delivery and fiscal receipts on Shopify?

Neither platform issues a Serbian fiscal receipt or collects cash at the door. We do both, independently of the platform.

Is there a test order before launch?

Yes. The store is connected at onboarding and a test order is run before the first shipment to customers.
