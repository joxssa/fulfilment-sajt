FAQ

Dropshipping fulfilment: frequently asked questions

Why local stock instead of shipping from China?

Serbian customers pay cash on delivery and expect delivery within days. Parcels from abroad cannot be paid at the door and take weeks, which kills conversion. With stock here, you sell like a local store.

Can you import the goods for me?

Yes. Through our assisted import from China, goods go from the factory straight into our warehouse on your Serbian company, received and barcoded. For smaller quantities, wholesale from our own stock is often simpler.

Do I need a company in Serbia?

Yes. Cash on delivery and fiscal receipts run through a company registered in Serbia. You open it, the stock sits in our warehouse on that company, and we ship and collect on its behalf.

Can I test a product with a small quantity?

Yes. Start with a small stock, see how the product sells with cash on delivery and scale the next order.
