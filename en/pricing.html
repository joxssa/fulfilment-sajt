Pricing

Fulfilment pricing in Serbia: what it depends on and what the quote contains

The price depends on the number of parcels, your product range, the storage space your goods occupy and the services you use. That is why we build quotes from concrete lines with clear units, VAT, validity and payment terms.

Units of calculation

You know what you pay for

Receiving and storage

Receiving per delivery or per item; storage by the space your goods occupy on a shelf, rack or pallet position.

Packing

Per parcel, with an add-on per extra item. Only the courier bag is included; boxes, stretch film, bubble wrap and filler are separate lines in the offer.

Couriers and cash on delivery

Courier cost per shipment by weight, dimensions and COD; COD handling and payout terms; returns as separate lines.

The quote

Every line has a unit, VAT and validity

A PAKUM quote lists every service you use with its price, unit of calculation, VAT, validity period and payment terms. Nothing is charged that is not in the quote. Certain cooperation packages also include our software for running Meta and TikTok ads; the terms are stated in the quote. FAQ

Pricing: frequently asked questions

Is there a public price list?

No, because the price depends on parcel volume, product range, storage space and services. Instead you get a quote where every line has a price, unit, VAT, validity and payment terms.

How is packing charged?

Per parcel, with an add-on per extra item in the order. Courier cost is shown separately per shipment by weight, dimensions and cash on delivery.

Is the software included?

Yes, TopOMS is included in fulfilment for clients. Store connection and onboarding are listed separately so you see what is included and what is charged.

Is there a start-up fee?

Any one-off costs, such as receiving and store connection, are listed in the quote and confirmed before the first delivery.
